Method & data

The question

Social protection programmes such as VUP (direct support, classic and expanded public works) and nutrition-sensitive direct support (NSDS) can miss poor households (exclusion error) and reach better-off ones (inclusion error). This tool measures both, by district, to support the NST2 commitment to reduce poverty with well-targeted social protection.

Data

  • NISR Integrated Household Living Conditions Survey (EICV): household consumption, official poverty status, and receipt of VUP components and NSDS (section 9D) for the same households. Ubudehe categories are not in the public microdata, and health insurance records do not show whether Mutuelle premiums were subsidised, so neither is counted as support.
  • Night-time lights: NOAA VIIRS Day/Night Band monthly composites (stray-light corrected), via Google Earth Engine. Pixels with fewer than 2 cloud-free nights are excluded, and district-months with under half the area observable are flagged.
  • Population: WorldPop 100 m, 2024 (R2025A), via Google Earth Engine.
  • District boundaries: geoBoundaries, CC BY 4.0.

Only aggregated district estimates and the trained model are published. No household microdata leaves the analysts' machines.

Indicators

  • Poverty rate: share of people in households below the national poverty line (person-weighted).
  • Exclusion error: share of poor households receiving no programme.
  • Inclusion error: share of recipient households that are not poor.

All estimates use EICV survey weights. 95% confidence intervals use Taylor linearisation over strata and clusters. Districts with fewer than 30 sampled poor households are flagged.

Household checker model

A gradient-boosted classifier trained on easy-to-observe household traits (housing, assets, demographics), with train and test sets split by survey cluster. {% if metrics %}Test AUC: {{ metrics.auc|floatformat:3 }} ({{ metrics.round }}).{% endif %}

Limitations

  • EICV is designed for district-level estimates. Sector-level figures would need small-area estimation.
  • Programme receipt is self-reported and may be under-reported.
  • Consumption poverty is one view of need. A household can be above the line and still vulnerable.
  • The checker is a screening aid. Final eligibility must follow official procedures and community validation.
